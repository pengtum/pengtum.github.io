The construction

Same outcome distribution, different mechanism

A method can look like it found regimes when it has only found high and low values. The benchmark removes that shortcut: under strict process heterogeneity the intercept is dropped and every regime's slope vector is normalized, so the regimes share the marginal distribution of Y and differ only in how X maps to Y.

Cross-regime transferability

A validity signal that needs no ground truth

Train inside one regime, predict in another. If the partition separates genuinely different mechanisms, that transfer should fail, so a strongly negative cross-region R² is evidence of real process boundaries rather than a defect. Values are truncated at −10.
