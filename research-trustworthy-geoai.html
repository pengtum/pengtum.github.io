Trustworthy GeoAI: Trust across the Spatial Analysis Chain

Uncertainty Quantification · Explainability · Visual Analytics
Overview

No GeoAI model is perfect. Its predictions are uncertain, its explanations can be unstable, and both vary across space. This research line asks how trust can carry through the whole chain of spatial analysis: from the data and the model, to its outputs, to how those outputs are communicated to decision-makers and the public.

For model outputs, GeoConformal prediction (GeoCP) gives distribution-free, spatially calibrated prediction intervals for any spatial model; GeoXCP extends uncertainty quantification to the explanations produced by explainable AI; GeoSIMCP handles nonstationary spatial processes; and Weighted Bayesian Conformal Prediction (WBCP) gives the exact Bayesian posterior over deployed risk under covariate shift. For communication, visual analytics and cartographic design make uncertainty and bias visible, so that a map shows not only what a model predicts but how far to trust it.
