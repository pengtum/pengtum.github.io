02 · The problem

One national cutoff misses the same lakes again

A national cutoff on EPA's weekly bloom probability, set to give 348 alerts per 1,000 observed lake-weeks, catches most bloom weeks. The map shows where its misses fall.

Misses repeat across seasons. Under EPA's own rule, a probability of 0.10, the cross-season reliability of each lake's miss residual was 0.64 (0.52–0.74), against 0.44 under a calibrated benchmark of independent outcomes; the national cutoff shown here gave 0.58.

03 · Florida

Where the misses pile up

In Florida the national cutoff missed 119 bloom weeks at 30 lakes, 10 of them in both seasons.

Repeatedly under-protected lakes cluster in Florida, along the Gulf coast and in California and Oregon. The 53 lakes that EPA's rule protected worst in 2025 had a hit rate of 0.79 in 2026 under that rule.

2026 hit rate under EPA's rule for ten groups of lakes ranked by their 2025 miss residual; group 10 is the 53 worst-protected lakes.

04 · Why

The probabilities largely follow the lake and the week

The map shows, week by week, the lakes whose latest composite showed a bloom. Red ones were still left off the national list: their probability was below its cutoff.

A lake effect and a week effect explained 98.9% of the variance of the logit of EPA's snapshot probabilities in 2025–26. When the latest composite showed a bloom (12,308 lake-weeks), the probabilities averaged 0.61 while 88% were bloom weeks; even among those below 0.05, 56% were.

Mean EPA probability against the share of lake-weeks in bloom, by the state of the latest composite: bloom (yellow), part of the lake over the line (teal), no signal (grey). Dashed: perfect calibration.

05 · How LakeSentry decides

Two readings of each lake's satellite record

The map shows LakeSentry's list week by week: yellow lakes are listed for their latest image, teal lakes for their history in that month.

1

Latest image

List the lake if at least a tenth of its valid pixels were above the bloom line in its latest composite.

2

Same-month history

Otherwise list it if its share of bloom weeks in this calendar month of past years reaches one national cutoff: 0.031 at 348 alerts per 1,000 lake-weeks. No forecast and no fitted model.

06 · LakeSentry

The same number of alerts, far fewer repeated misses

With as many alerts as the national cutoff, LakeSentry's misses on the map are fewer and rarely return.

115

110

In Florida it missed 4 bloom weeks at 3 lakes. The 53 lakes that EPA's rule protected worst in 2025 reached a 2026 hit rate of 0.996 under LakeSentry. In Lake Rosalie, Florida, EPA's rule missed 20 of 31 bloom weeks; LakeSentry missed none.

07 · Results

Fewer excess misses at the same number of alerts

Excess misses: missed bloom weeks beyond a tenth of each lake's bloom weeks, in weeks, with 95% HUC4-cluster bootstrap intervals; 348 alerts per 1,000 lake-weeks, EPA's rule at its own volume of 280.

With daily products and only the lake interior, LakeSentry left 136.7 fewer excess misses than the national cutoff (83.5–203.1). With 10% of lakes listed each week and older images, it could not be separated from the forecast with the same signal (−15.0 weeks, −34.8 to 4.2).

08 · In the water

The lakes it adds reach the bloom level more often

Share of laboratory samples with chlorophyll-a of at least 12 µg/L in lake-weeks listed only by the first list (coloured dot) or only by the second (grey dot).

Samples come from the Water Quality Portal, in lake-weeks listed by one list and not the other. The difference comes from which lakes are listed, not which weeks: within lakes sampled under both lists, no difference was detected. Samples lie near shore and do not identify cyanobacteria.

09 · Limits and the next test

What these results do not show yet

A replay

The results replay a historical EPA dashboard snapshot; first-issued values and input timing are not established.

Satellite-defined blooms

Bloom weeks come from the same product the list reads, and most extra catches lie near the bloom line.

Chosen after a look

LakeSentry became the main rule after its first comparisons on these two seasons.

The 2027 season

The unchanged rule will be tested on the 2027 season under a plan specified before the season begins.
