About this trace

This page replays, verbatim, the recorded evolution run visualized in Figure 4 of the paper: each round's system prompt, retrieved knowledge, best program, and logged metrics exactly as produced by the run. The selection fitness (combined_score) is the mean Rand Index over the three training grids (Rectangular #5, Voronoi #75, Arbitrary #149), computed by a frozen evaluation section that lies outside the EVOLVE-BLOCK; the SSR, NMI, and MAE fields in the log are recorded for monitoring. The evolvable algorithm block references no ground-truth labels in any round, and the discovered algorithm's own internal objective is label-free (post-merge SSR under a connectivity constraint), so it runs unchanged on real data. File-system paths in the trace are redacted to <PROJECT_ROOT>.
