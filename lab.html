TrustGeoAI · University of Iowa

Trustworthy GeoAI Lab

We build GeoAI that knows where it can be wrong. GeoAI now informs decisions about cities, health, and the environment, yet every model is uncertain, and that uncertainty varies across space. The lab develops spatially explicit, explainable, and uncertainty-aware methods so that maps made by models can be trusted, and so that users know where they cannot.

Research
01 Spatially explicit modeling Models that account for spatial heterogeneity and geographic context in prediction and association analysis. 02 Trustworthy GeoAI Explainable AI and uncertainty quantification that show how reliable each prediction is, and where. 03 Transferable & scalable GeoAI Methods that generalize across regions, datasets, and tasks, including LLM agents for geospatial model discovery.
