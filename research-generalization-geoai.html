AI for Geospatial Discovery

LLM Agents · Automated Model Discovery · GeoAI Foundations
Overview

Most geospatial methods are still designed by hand: a researcher proposes a model, tests it, and revises it. This research line asks whether AI can take part in that loop, using large language model agents that propose, implement, test, and refine geospatial models and algorithms, guided by geographic knowledge and evaluated against real spatial data.

GeoEvolve uses multi-agent large language models to automate geospatial model discovery. SEGA (Self-Evolving Geospatial Algorithms) applies an LLM-driven framework to spatial regime discovery, letting algorithms evolve and be checked against spatial benchmarks. Related work examines the mathematical foundations and open challenges of GeoAI, and treats buildings as text for universal building attribute regression.

Key Contributions
  • SEGA: self-evolving geospatial algorithms, with a case study on spatial regime discovery
  • GeoEvolve: automating geospatial model discovery via multi-agent large language models (arXiv 2025)
  • Mathematical foundations and challenges for GeoAI (book chapter, IOS Press 2026)
  • Buildings as Text: a universal regression paradigm for building attribute prediction (EGU 2026)
Software
