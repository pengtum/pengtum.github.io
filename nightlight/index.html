Where does night-time light matter for population?

Each dot is one 100 m grid cell in Munich (6,000 of 29,520 shown), colored by its SHAP value for night-time light.

  1. Little, almost everywhereAcross most cells, night-time light adds little: the model leans on built form instead.
  2. But not at randomWhen the dots leave the plot for the map, the exceptions are not scattered at random.
  3. Structured in spaceThe strong negative contributions concentrate in specific parts of the city.

From P. Luo, C. Chen, S. Gao, X. Zhang, D. Majok Chol, Z. Yang, L. Meng. Understanding of the predictability and uncertainty in population distributions empowered by visual analytics. International Journal of Geographical Information Science, 39(3), 675–705, 2025. Read the paper →
